のり寿司

おはなし Story

Every morning Nori-chan rolls up the shutter of her tiny sushi shop, ties on her headband, and lays out the day’s plates. Every morning Koinu — her very round, very hungry puppy — rolls right out the door after them.

Down the lantern-lit street he goes, korokoro, korokoro, scooping up runaway nigiri while dodging grumpy wasabi and the yatai carts of the morning market. Help him bring home as much sushi as he can before the shop opens!
