おはなし Story

Every morning Nori-chan opens her tiny sushi shop, ties on her headband and sets out the day’s plates. And every morning a few pieces of sushi slip off the counter and roll out the door. Koinu, her very round and very hungry puppy, rolls right after them.

Down the lantern-lit street he goes, korokoro, korokoro, scooping up runaway nigiri and dodging grumpy wasabi and the market’s yatai carts. Help him bring back as much sushi as he can before the shop opens!

Combos

Every 5 sushi in a row adds +0.5× to your multiplier, up to ×4. Bumping into anything — or going 2.4 seconds without a bite — resets the chain.
